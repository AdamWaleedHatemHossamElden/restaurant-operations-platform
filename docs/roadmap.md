# Roadmap

1. **Phase 1 — Foundation:** project structure, React/Spring Boot foundations, MySQL and Flyway, security defaults, health checks, test tooling, and architecture documentation.
2. **Phase 2 — Authentication and roles:** backend and frontend authentication are merged, including rotating refresh sessions, memory-only access tokens, protected routes, and safe logout. Later user-management slices remain pending.
3. **Phase 3 — Restaurant tables and reservations:** Phase 3A table management and Phase 3B reservation management are merged into `main`, including UTC scheduling, live suitability, serialized overlap protection, optimistic locking, controlled transitions, audit events, and responsive workspaces.
4. **Phase 4 — Menu and order management:** Phase 4A menu configuration and Phase 4B order management are merged into `main`, including table-service order capture, immutable pricing snapshots, exact totals, controlled lifecycle transitions, and chronological status history.
5. **Phase 5 — Kitchen display and real-time updates:** merged, including transactional kitchen tickets, item preparation, derived queue state, READY-gated completion, authenticated WebSocket/STOMP notifications, and REST recovery after reconnect.
6. **Phase 6 — Inventory, recipes, suppliers, and purchasing:** merged into `main`, including the immutable stock ledger, low-stock visibility, kitchen recipe consumption, supplier pricing, purchase orders, partial receiving, and concurrency protection.
7. **Phase 7 — Staff scheduling:** merged into `main`, including employee records, scheduling-domain operational roles, date-specific availability, conflict-safe shifts, controlled lifecycle, audit, and responsive weekly views.
8. **Phase 8 — Payments and invoices:** merged into `main`, including confirmed EUR payment recording, partial and split settlement, idempotency and overpayment protection, immutable reconciliation and invoices, audit, and responsive settlement views. Provider integration, refunds, taxes, discounts, and tips remain outside the implemented scope.
9. **Phase 9 — Reports and analytics:** current unmerged work on `phase-9-reports-analytics`, including ADMIN-only read projections, bounded half-open periods, exact operational metrics, UTC time series, responsive summaries, and safe CSV exports.
10. **Phase 10 — UI/UX redesign and product polish:** a dedicated frontend/product-design phase after all major business functionality. It will establish visual identity, color, typography, spacing, icons, navigation/sidebar, dashboard, reusable design-system components, and consistent responsive and accessible buttons, forms, tables, dialogs, cards, charts, and states. Phase 10 is not implemented by Phase 9.
11. **Phase 11 — Final testing, production hardening, deployment, and portfolio presentation:** full regression testing, CI/CD, deployment and production configuration, secret management, dependency scanning, observability, readiness, final accessibility review, documentation, screenshots, GitHub presentation, and portfolio narrative. Phase 11 is not implemented by Phase 9.

Each phase should ship tested vertical behavior without claiming later modules are complete.
